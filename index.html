NonLagMA EA is a MetaTrader MT4/MT5 Expert Advisor designed for trend trading. It trades with the market trend, uses hedging and martingale strategies, trails winning trades, and recovers losses from losing trades through advanced hedging techniques.

NonLagMA main functions

  • Detects the market trend using the NonLagMA indicator color, price position, or both
  • Enters or exits trades/baskets based on the NonLagMA indicator signals
  • Trails winning trades, manages stop-loss, break-even, lot sizes, and martingale strategies
  • Hedges losses using recovery zones or with the trend; classic stop-loss is also supported

This MetaTrader NonLagMA Expert Advisor (EA) is a powerful trend trading tool for traders who follow the "trend is your friend" strategy. Similar to the Fluid Expert Advisor, it can detect trends, open trades in the trend direction, hedge positions, and recover losses using the Auto Recovery algorithm. Standard trade management features like stop-loss, break-even, and trailing stop are fully supported.

NonLagMA EA trades with the trend: buys in uptrend and sells in downtrend

The trend is determined by the NonLagMA indicator color, the price position relative to the NonLagMA, or a combination of both (see image above). The EA closes trades or trade baskets at the NonLagMA signal if the profit is higher than or equal to the defined MinProfit.

Trade examples

To trade effectively with the NonLagMA Expert Advisor, traders must first select a trend detection method. This can be based on the NonLagMA indicator color (black = sell, white = buy), price position relative to the NonLagMA (price above = buy, price below = sell), or a combination of both criteria.

The second NonLagMA indicator determines trade entries and can also be used for closing trades or trade baskets. Exiting at a NonLagMA reverse signal ensures trades are closed before a trend reversal occurs.

Experienced traders can enable the martingale option to double or triple the next trade lot if the previous trade was a loss.

How to choose hedging settings

How do you determine your manual GMT offset?
Go to https://greenwichmeantime.com/ Find your brokers time by seeing it displayed in your trading platform under Market Watch (the top left hand side of your MetaTrader4 platform). Now do a little math to find out the hourly difference between the GMT and your brokers time. That is the offset. If the market watch time is ahead of the GMT then your setting is a positive number. But don't use a plus sign. Just write as you normally would for a positive number. If your broker's time is behind the GMT put a negative sign in front of the number.
